## Findings

No primary-source evidence found of an acute customer-demand break. The March Meta 6-K is materially positive demand evidence, with long-duration contracted capacity beginning in 2027 and a large unsold-capacity backstop. The July 15 asset-light release also argues demand remains sufficient for Nebius to invite third-party infrastructure capital into its capacity pool.

No primary-source evidence found of a fresh funding-access failure. The Q1 balance sheet showed large cash resources, and the March NVIDIA warrant plus convertible notes materially expanded funding capacity. The risk is not "no access to capital"; it is whether Nebius can convert very large committed capex and partner capacity into durable returns without excessive dilution, debt burden, or execution slippage.

No primary-source evidence found of a fresh utilization disclosure break. The company has not provided enough utilization or backlog-to-revenue conversion detail to clear the valuation question. The dislocation therefore keeps a real evidence gap around cash conversion and timing, especially because major Meta capacity starts early 2027 while Q1 2026 capex was already very large.

Jurisdiction and geopolitical risk remain live but not newly company-specific. The 20-F explicitly flags Israel/regional conflict exposure, and the local middle_east regime heartbeat worsened on July 16. That is a risk amplifier for sizing and execution, not evidence that Nebius's customer contracts, power access, or cloud operations have broken.

The July 16 move is best bounded as a valuation/crowding/cash-conversion repricing of a capital-intensive AI cloud compounder after a large rerating, amid broad AI-infrastructure risk-off. It is not currently supported by primary evidence of a broken AI cloud demand thesis.

## Remaining uncertainty

- Utilization and backlog conversion remain insufficiently transparent for a BUY_NOW or ADD_ON_DIP decision.
- 2026-2027 capex intensity, partner-model economics, and customer concentration need PM-level valuation work.
- Regional conflict and grid/regulatory risk should remain explicit sizing constraints.
- Any new 6-K, customer termination/modification, financing repricing, or SA position change should reopen the source gap.

## PM decision item

Portfolio PM must decide whether NBIS remains RESEARCH_REQUIRED until a valuation/backlog-conversion model is refreshed, or moves back to HOLD/watch with explicit no-action sizing discipline. This memo does not authorize BUY_NOW, ADD_ON_DIP, TRIM, or SELL.

## Resolution basis

The dip-trigger source gap is resolved only as to cause: the July 13-17 dislocations appear better explained by capex/cash-conversion concern, valuation/crowding pressure, broad neocloud/AI-infrastructure risk-off, and macro/regime risk than by a newly discovered primary-source operating break.
