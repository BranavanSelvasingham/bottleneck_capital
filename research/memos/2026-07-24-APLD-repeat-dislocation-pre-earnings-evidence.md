## Finding

The reopened APLD source gap is bounded through 2026-07-24. I found no new primary-source
evidence after the July 16 memo of a customer cancellation, lease invalidation, construction
failure, financing default, liquidity warning, exchange notice, power-site failure, or
guidance withdrawal. The latest company operating evidence still points to on-schedule
Polaris Forge 1 delivery, while the latest material financing evidence confirms the thesis
remains highly capital-intensive and structurally complex.

The repeat dislocations are best explained as renewed valuation and financing-risk repricing
ahead of the July 27 FY2026 results, layered on broad AI-infrastructure crowding, rates,
energy/shipping, tariff, and grid-regulatory stress. That is not benign: APLD still has
material debt, preferred-equity/dilution overhang, parent completion obligations, customer
concentration, project timing, and power/regulatory exposure. But the refreshed source check
does not identify a fresh APLD-specific thesis break.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- July 27 FY2026 results must update cash, restricted cash, debt, capex, construction
  cadence, PEPA usage, preferred-equity dilution, project-level funding needs, and segment
  economics.
- Portfolio PM still needs valuation work using enterprise value, contracted base-term
  revenue, financing obligations, preferred-equity dilution, time-to-cash-flow, and
  completion-guarantee risk.
- Customer concentration, CoreWeave/hyperscaler credit support, power availability,
  interconnection/regulatory risk, equipment/procurement cost inflation, and tariff exposure
  remain unresolved decision inputs.
