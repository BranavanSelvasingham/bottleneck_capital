## Primary-source evidence

### June 22 8-K - note offering launch

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. filed a June 22, 2026 Form 8-K reporting:

- Updated cash and cash equivalents of approximately $100.8 billion as of June 19, 2026, furnished to prospective note investors.
- Launch of a senior unsecured notes offering to qualified institutional buyers under Rule 144A and non-U.S. buyers under Regulation S.
- Intended use of proceeds: repay bridge-loan borrowings in full, pay fees and expenses, and use any remainder for general corporate purposes.
- Notes were not registered under the Securities Act and the 8-K warned against relying on June 19 cash as the June 30 balance.

Source: SEC 8-K, filed June 22, 2026: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026044489/spcx8-kxlaunchseniornotes.htm

### June 23 pricing release

SpaceX priced $25.0 billion of senior unsecured notes across five maturities:

- $7.0 billion 5.350% Senior Notes due 2031.
- $6.0 billion 5.650% Senior Notes due 2033.
- $6.0 billion 5.875% Senior Notes due 2036.
- $2.5 billion 6.600% Senior Notes due 2046.
- $3.5 billion 6.650% Senior Notes due 2056.

The company repeated that proceeds were intended to repay the bridge loan in full, cover related costs, and support general corporate purposes.

Source: SpaceX investor release, June 23, 2026: https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/SpaceX-Announces-Pricing-of-25-Billion-Inaugural-Bond-Issuance-2026-33VwNgsx3O/default.aspx

### June 26 8-K - issuance and registration rights

The June 26 8-K confirms the notes were issued under an indenture with The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A. as trustee. The notes are unsecured obligations and rank equally with existing and future unsubordinated indebtedness. Interest is semiannual beginning January 15, 2027. SpaceX also agreed to use commercially reasonable efforts to complete a registered exchange offer no later than 540 days after issue date.

Source: SEC 8-K, filed June 26, 2026: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026045763/spcx-closing8xkjune2026.htm

### Credit-rating frame

S&P assigned the proposed senior unsecured notes a BBB issue-level rating with a stable outlook. The rating case expected adjusted leverage below 2.0x even while SpaceX invests aggressively and generates significantly negative free cash flow. That supports the view that the note transaction reduced near-term refinancing risk, but it does not remove the core anti-thesis around capital intensity and cash burn.

Source: S&P Global Ratings research update, June 22, 2026: https://www.spglobal.com/ratings/en/regulatory/article/-/view/type/HTML/id/3584128
