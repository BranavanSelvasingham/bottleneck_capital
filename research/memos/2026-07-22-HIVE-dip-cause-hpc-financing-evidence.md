HIVE's July 2026 dislocations are bounded as miner-to-datacenter execution, financing/dilution, bitcoin-beta, and crowded-AI-infrastructure repricing under elevated macro/geopolitical risk. ## What appears resolved

- The source gap is no longer "HIVE has only AI optionality with no contract evidence." HIVE now has reported HPC revenue, contracted ARR, a named Bell/Cohere GPU-cloud arrangement, an additional Sweden colocation LOI, and financing earmarked partly for GPUs and data-center development.
- The July dislocations do not map to a found primary-source customer cancellation, guidance withdrawal, liquidity default, exchange notice, site loss, or official SA exit/reduction.
- The most likely cause is repricing of a still-early conversion story: mining remains the current revenue base, Q4 mining economics and adjusted EBITDA were pressured, major HPC upside depends on late-2026/2027 deployments, the Boden LOI is non-binding, and HIVE added exchangeable-note and ATM-style equity-financing complexity shortly before the dislocations.

## Remaining uncertainty

- Mining still dominates current revenue: FY2026 digital-currency revenue was far larger than HPC revenue, and Q4 adjusted EBITDA was negative under lower bitcoin prices and higher network difficulty.
- The Bell/Cohere contract is material but not yet operating at steady state; the disclosed go-live window is late 2026 to early 2027.
- The Boden LOI is not a definitive lease. Retrofit cost, timing, customer identity, final pricing, and execution risk remain unresolved.
- The 320 MW Greater Toronto Area AI gigafactory and broader ARR targets remain forward-looking and capital-intensive; PM needs funding, dilution, return-on-capital, power, permitting, and delivery evidence before any capital action.
- Exchangeable-note financing improves near-term funding access but adds future exchange/dilution and hedge-flow complexity. It is not a thesis break by itself, but it matters for valuation and sizing.
- The local filing ingest gap remains operationally material even though this pass used direct SEC/web primary sources for HIVE.
