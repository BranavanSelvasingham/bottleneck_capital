# BITF / KEEL Identity and Dip Evidence

## Question

BITF is already marked `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` because live market data now maps the former Bitfarms exposure to Keel Infrastructure (`KEEL`). The active signal queue has five high-priority July dip triggers for BITF/KEEL. The research task is to determine whether this is a broken mapping/source gap or a still-valid miner-to-datacenter candidate whose dislocation can be bounded for Portfolio PM review.

This memo does not make a portfolio decision and does not authorize BUY_NOW, ADD_ON_DIP, TRIM, or SELL.

## Evidence Reviewed

- Keel / Bitfarms April 1, 2026 rebrand and U.S. redomiciliation release: Bitfarms completed the statutory arrangement, Keel became the ultimate parent, former Bitfarms shareholders received one Keel common share for each Bitfarms share, and Keel common stock was expected to trade on Nasdaq and TSX under `KEEL` in substitution for Bitfarms shares.
- Keel investor overview checked July 20, 2026: Keel describes itself as an infrastructure-first owner/developer of data centers and energy infrastructure for HPC workloads including AI, with 2.2 GW of total pipeline and `KEEL` as the Nasdaq/TSX ticker.
- Keel Q1 2026 results release, May 11, 2026: management framed the redomiciliation/rebrand as the completion of a nearly two-year transformation from Bitcoin mining toward North American HPC/AI infrastructure; near-term work is Panther Creek, Sharon, and Moses Lake through lease execution.
- Keel June 5/9, 2026 convertible-note releases: Keel priced and closed $458 million of 1.25% convertible senior notes due 2032, using capped calls and keeping proceeds available for long-lead equipment deposits, letters of credit, and accelerating development projects. The company said existing liquidity was expected to be sufficient to develop Panther Creek, Sharon, and Moses Lake through leasing.
- Keel July 15, 2026 Sherbrooke update: city approval allows Keel to enter agreements with Hydro-Sherbrooke for transfer/operation of 96 MW of existing capacity and recategorization from BTC mining to HPC/AI, subject to Quebec MEIE review for the power transfer.
- SEC Form 10-Q for Keel, quarter ended March 31, 2026: Keel is the successor issuer to Bitfarms, describes itself as a North American digital and energy infrastructure company developing data centers and energy infrastructure for HPC/AI workloads, and still maintains legacy Bitcoin mining operations. The filing shows ongoing losses from continuing operations, restricted cash tied to letters of credit, and material development/regulatory/financing/customer risks.
- Local regime context through July 20: adverse Middle East, rates, financing-liquidity, and grid-regulation signals remain active. These matter because the miner-to-datacenter sleeve is highly exposed to financing liquidity, energy input, BTC beta, customer concentration, and execution risk.

## Findings

The identity/source gap is now bounded: the old BITF exposure maps to Keel Infrastructure (`KEEL`) through a one-for-one redomiciliation/rebrand, not a stale unrelated quote. Primary Keel and SEC evidence supports keeping this as a valid research mapping for the former Bitfarms public exposure.

The thesis fit is also not broken by identity alone. Keel is explicitly pivoting from Bitcoin mining toward HPC/AI infrastructure, has a stated 2.2 GW pipeline, and is advancing named projects at Panther Creek, Sharon, Moses Lake, and Sherbrooke. The July 15 Sherbrooke release is directly relevant to miner-to-datacenter conversion because it concerns reusing 96 MW of existing mining capacity for HPC/AI.

The dip cause is bounded, but not cleared as buyable. The July dislocations are more plausibly explained by dilution/convertible-overhang, project execution timing, lack of signed lease/customer economics on the named Keel campuses, legacy Bitcoin-mining losses and BTC beta, Quebec approval risk, broader AI-infrastructure crowding, and macro financing/energy risk. I found no primary-source evidence of a fresh July customer cancellation, lease failure, liquidity default, delisting issue, governance break, or definitive proof that Keel no longer expresses the intended miner-to-datacenter thesis.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- Lease/customer economics for Panther Creek, Sharon, Moses Lake, and Sherbrooke are not yet primary-source proven.
- Sherbrooke still needs MEIE review for the power transfer.
- The June convertible financing improves flexibility but creates dilution/overhang and does not by itself prove project returns.
- Legacy mining operations and Bitcoin beta remain material until HPC/AI leasing becomes economically visible.
- Portfolio PM still needs to decide whether BITF/KEEL remains `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` pending lease economics and valuation work, or whether the identity gap is sufficiently resolved to move it into HOLD/watch under existing no-action discipline.

Decide whether to update the public research files from `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` due to identity mismatch toward a HOLD/watch framework for `BITF` mapped to `KEEL`, or keep `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` until primary-source customer leases, project economics, Quebec approval, and dilution-adjusted valuation are available.

No buy, add, trim, or sell decision is made in this memo.
