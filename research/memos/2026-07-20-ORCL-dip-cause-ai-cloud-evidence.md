## Evidence conclusion

The ORCL dislocation cause is bounded as an AI-infrastructure funding, free-cash-flow, credit, customer-concentration, and valuation reset. Primary Oracle sources do not show a fresh operating miss, guidance cut, OCI demand break, RPO contraction, or filing-disclosed liquidity crisis. They do show exactly why the equity is under pressure: negative free cash flow, very large capex, debt/equity funding needs, long RPO conversion duration, and concentrated AI infrastructure execution risk.

The SA-reported put signal should remain a caution about crowded AI downside and funding/counterparty risk. It is not a short instruction and does not by itself decide the long thesis.

## Remaining uncertainty

- RPO quality and conversion timing remain critical because only a small portion converts over the next twelve months.
- OpenAI/large-customer concentration, datacenter lease obligations, and customer ability to pay remain material.
- Negative free cash flow and new financing needs can continue to pressure equity even if demand is real.
- The June 2026 credit downgrade is not an operating break, but it is a genuine warning that the balance-sheet cushion is thinner.
- This memo does not prove valuation is attractive after the drop.
