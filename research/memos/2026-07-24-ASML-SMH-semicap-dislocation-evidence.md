## Resolver conclusion

The July 15-17 ASML and SMH dislocations are bounded as broad semicap / crowded-AI-beta repricing, valuation pressure, export-control/geopolitical risk, and hedge-pressure noise rather than a confirmed ASML-specific thesis break.

ASML's same-day primary Q2 2026 package points the other direction operationally: Q2 sales and gross margin were above guidance, full-year 2026 revenue and margin guidance were raised, customer commitments/order momentum were described as very strong, Low NA EUV and DUV immersion capacity expansion was planned for 2027 with possible further 2028 expansion, and High NA EUV reached an Intel 18A production-readiness milestone. I did not find primary-source evidence of a guidance withdrawal, order/backlog deterioration, customer cancellation, liquidity/default event, exchange notice, or export-control change that newly impairs the core EUV runway.

This does not clear ASML for capital action. The Portfolio PM still needs valuation, sizing, export-control, China mix, customer capex digestion, and SA put-signal interpretation before any decision change. SMH remains signal-only and should not be treated as a put, short, or ETF trade instruction.

## Interpretation

The ASML evidence directly addresses the asset file's open questions on order/backlog durability and EUV demand. The July 15 dislocation occurred into a stronger official operating update, not a weaker one. The most important thesis-positive items are raised full-year guidance, higher Q3 guidance, strong first-half order momentum, increasing backlog, near-full 2027 Low NA EUV order coverage, meaningful 2028 orders, and Intel production use of High NA on 18A.

The bear case is still real but not newly proven. Export controls and China exposure remain material because China is expected to be about 20% of 2026 sales and ASML's own risk language highlights license and shipment restrictions. Customer capex digestion remains a risk because the improved guide relies on customers executing aggressive multi-year AI logic and memory capacity plans. Valuation/crowding risk remains acute because SMH is heavily exposed to the same AI semiconductor complex and the local market log shows synchronized semicap/AI-beta dislocations.

For SMH, the signal is useful only as a broad semiconductor crowding and hedge-pressure marker. It does not create a long-only ETF thesis and does not authorize puts or shorts.
