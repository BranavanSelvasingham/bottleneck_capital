## What changed since the unresolved signal

Instead, the signal cluster is best bounded as crowded AI-beta repricing, valuation stress after a large 2026 rerating, execution-timing risk, financing/circularity concern around AI customer support, and macro/geopolitical risk.

Primary evidence against an immediate operating break:

- AMD reported Q1 revenue of $10.3B, GAAP gross margin of 53%, non-GAAP gross margin of 55%, GAAP operating income of $1.5B, and non-GAAP operating income of $2.5B. Data Center revenue was $5.8B, up 57% year over year, driven by EPYC demand and continued Instinct GPU ramp.
- AMD guided Q2 2026 revenue to about $11.2B plus/minus $300M and non-GAAP gross margin of about 56%. Q2 results are scheduled for 2026-08-04 after close.

- AMD and Anthropic announced up to 2 GW of MI450 Series GPUs in Helios rackscale solutions, with first-gigawatt deployment beginning in 1H 2027, plus engineering collaboration around Claude, Instinct, and ROCm.
- The AMD filing index shows the latest listed July filings are Form 4 / Form 144 insider-sale mechanics and a July 1 Form 8-K about executive compensation changes. The July 1 8-K is not a customer, guidance, liquidity, litigation, resignation, delisting, or covenant-break filing.
