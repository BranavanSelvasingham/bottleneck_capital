## Bounded cause of the dislocation

The July dislocation does not appear to be caused by a disclosed customer cancellation, CoreWeave non-payment, guidance withdrawal, covenant default, exchange notice, site loss, or official SA exit/reduction.

The better bounded explanation is:

- financing and leverage repricing after the $3.3 billion secured-note transaction and project completion guarantee;
- execution-timing risk on Muskogee, Pecos, Hunt County, and broader 4.5 GW gross-power pipeline conversion;
- customer-concentration risk, with CoreWeave-funded capex and existing colocation agreements central to near-term HDC revenue;
- uncertainty about leasing uncommitted future capacity at attractive returns;
- residual bitcoin-mining exposure and power/curtailment sensitivity;
- macro/geopolitical pressure from elevated rates, Middle East energy/shipping risk, and U.S. grid-reliability scrutiny;
- broad crowded-AI infrastructure repricing across miners, neoclouds, power, and semiconductor names.

## Remaining uncertainty

This memo does not resolve valuation or capital allocation. The PM still needs:

- Q2 delivery proof for the 70 MW Muskogee GB300 building and whether it moved from commissioning to customer revenue without delay.
- Evidence of additional contracted customers beyond the existing concentrated relationship.
- Full use-of-proceeds, pro forma debt-service, lien, and completion-guarantee economics after the notes closed.
- Capex-to-cash-flow bridge for Muskogee, Pecos, Hunt County, and other pipeline projects.
- Customer-funded capex reimbursement/cash conversion cadence.
- Sensitivity to curtailment, utility rates, gas/oil shock, and grid-reliability requirements.
- Updated valuation against contracted HDC revenue, unleased pipeline, debt, warrants, and residual mining assets.
