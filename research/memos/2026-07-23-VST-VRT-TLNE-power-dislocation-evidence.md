## Findings

No primary-source evidence found in this pass shows a fresh company-specific thesis break for VST, VRT, or TLNE/TLN.

VST evidence remains operationally supportive, but not risk-free. Vistra's Q1 release reported Q1 ongoing operations adjusted EBITDA of $1.494 billion, reaffirmed 2026 ongoing operations adjusted EBITDA guidance of $6.8 billion to $7.6 billion and FCFbG guidance of $3.925 billion to $4.725 billion, and disclosed high hedge coverage of expected generation volumes for 2026-2028. The same release noted Meta long-term PPAs and pending Cogentrix contribution are not included in the reaffirmed ranges. The latest Vistra news page shows Q2 results are scheduled for August 7, 2026; I found no later primary guidance withdrawal, PPA impairment, liquidity/default notice, outage disclosure, or deal failure.

VRT evidence supports the AI power/thermal equipment thesis, while leaving valuation and order-normalization risk unresolved. Vertiv's Q1 release reported 30% year-over-year net sales growth, adjusted operating margin of 20.8%, adjusted free cash flow of $653 million, approximately 0.2x net leverage, and raised full-year 2026 guidance to $13.5 billion-$14.0 billion net sales and $6.30-$6.40 adjusted diluted EPS. Vertiv's May investor conference framed the business around converged infrastructure for the AI era, and the June ThermoKey closing expanded heat-rejection capacity for AI data centers. The latest Q2 release date is July 29, 2026; I found no primary order cancellation, customer loss, margin guide cut, liquidity stress, or exchange notice.

TLNE/TLN evidence is mixed but bounded. Talen's Q1 release reaffirmed 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $1.75 billion-$2.05 billion and adjusted free cash flow guidance of $980 million-$1.18 billion, while also describing acquisition financing and interest-savings expectations. Talen then closed the Western PJM asset acquisition and, on July 14, reported 10,180 MW cleared in the PJM 2028/2029 auction at $325/MW-day, equating to about $1.208 billion of capacity revenue for that planning year. Its Q2 results are due after the August 5 close. I found no primary evidence of Susquehanna/AWS impairment, customer cancellation, guidance withdrawal, liquidity default, or regulatory block. The ticker/source alias remains a local-system issue: company primary sources use NASDAQ `TLN`, while local files and signals use `TLNE`.

The likely dislocation cause is broad risk repricing, not confirmed company-specific thesis damage: AI/power equities sold off into confirmed energy/shipping escalation, elevated rates/inflation risk, data-center grid-reliability rulemaking, and crowded AI beta pressure. That broad cause is consistent with simultaneous VRT, VST, TLNE, SMH, ASML, TSM, MU, NVDA, SNDK, and other AI-infrastructure dislocations in the local event log.
