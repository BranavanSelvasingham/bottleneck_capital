## Interpretation

The primary evidence supports ONTO as an advanced-packaging/process-control proxy: HBM4 inspection commitments, 2.5D logic qualification, advanced-node metrology wins, Semilab/Rigaku portfolio expansion, and Q2 revenue guidance above Q1. That evidence directly addresses the asset file's open question about whether advanced packaging and process control are real demand drivers.

The counterweight is not thesis break; it is risk quality. Onto is still an adjacent proxy rather than a current SA-reported 13F holding. The $710 million Rigaku stake and $1.5 billion convertible financing improve strategic breadth and funding availability, but they also create integration, valuation, dilution, and capital-allocation questions. Q1 GAAP operating income was materially below the prior-year quarter despite revenue growth, so Q2 must confirm margin conversion, order durability, and whether advanced-packaging strength is broad enough to offset cyclicality.
