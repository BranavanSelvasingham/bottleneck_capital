# ORCL - Oracle

## Current Decision

**BUY_NOW, half starter at $138-$145.** At the July 10 price of $140.64, Oracle trades at
approximately 17.5 times FY2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance of $8.05. That is attractive relative to
93% Q4 OCI growth and a $638 billion remaining-performance-obligation backlog.

This is an independent long thesis. Situational Awareness LP's reported put remains a warning
about capex duration, crowding, leverage, and downside, not an instruction to short or avoid
researching the long.

## Primary Evidence

- Q4 revenue was $19.2 billion, up 21%; cloud revenue was $9.9 billion, up 47%.
- Q4 IaaS revenue was $5.8 billion, up 93%.
- Remaining performance obligations reached $638 billion, up 363% year over year.
- Oracle disclosed $75 billion of customer-prepaid or customer-supplied AI hardware.
- FY2027 guidance calls for $90 billion revenue and $8.05 non-GAAP EPS.
- FY2026 free cash flow was negative $23.7 billion, and FY2027 funding plans include debt and
  equity issuance. The discount is therefore real, but it is not low risk.

## Variant View

The market focuses on the financing burden and customer concentration. The variant is that
prepayments and contracted demand shorten the duration of Oracle's infrastructure investment,
allowing earnings to compound faster than the forward multiple suggests.
