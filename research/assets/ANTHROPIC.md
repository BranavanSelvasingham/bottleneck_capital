# ANTHROPIC - Anthropic PBC

## Current Decision

**RESEARCH_REQUIRED / NOT TRADABLE.** Anthropic confirmed on June 1 that it confidentially
submitted a draft S-1. It has not announced a public prospectus, ticker, exchange, price range, or
offering size. A September 4 Reuters report says the expected timetable moved to a late-September
prospectus and mid-October marketing at the earliest. Treat that timetable as reporting, not a
company commitment.

## Why It Matters

Anthropic is a direct frontier-AI platform candidate and a major demand read-through for the
portfolio's compute, custom silicon, networking, memory, cloud, and power theses. Its IPO filings
should expose economics that private revenue headlines cannot: revenue quality, compute-adjusted
gross margin, cash burn, contracted capacity, customer concentration, and financing duration.

## Required IPO Underwriting

1. Audited revenue and growth, gross margin after compute, operating loss, and free cash flow.
2. Cloud, chip, power, lease, purchase, and financing commitments.
3. Customer, channel, and compute-provider concentration.
4. Fully diluted shares and enterprise value across the proposed range.
5. Primary versus secondary proceeds, overallotment, strategic holders, lockups, and unlocks.
6. Governance, public-benefit structure, related parties, regulation, litigation, and safety risk.
7. Competition from OpenAI, Google, Meta, xAI, and lower-cost open-weight models.
8. Public float, index eligibility, brokerage access, and post-listing price discovery.

## Public-Market Playbook

- Do not buy an unverified proxy or treat allocation scarcity as intrinsic value.
- Do not commit to an entry price until fully diluted share count and audited economics are public.
- After listing, require a bear/base/bull valuation, a named invalidation, and staged sizing.
- Recheck read-through effects on AVGO, AMD, MU, TSM, MRVL, CRWV, ORCL, and SPCX only when
  filings show actual counterparties, commitments, or demand economics.
