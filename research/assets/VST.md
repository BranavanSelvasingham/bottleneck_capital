# VST - Vistra

## Current Decision

**BUY_NOW, but no more than half the intended allocation at $152-$160.** The July 10 price of
$158.86 values the common equity at approximately $53.6 billion. Midpoint 2026 adjusted
free-cash-flow-before-growth is $4.325 billion, or an 8.1% headline yield. After roughly
$192 million of annual preferred dividends, the yield is closer to 7.7%, and it still excludes
growth spending.

This is the best current new-name entry because it diversifies the AI thesis into the power
bottleneck. The current geopolitical regime is elevated, but higher energy risk is less adverse
to Vistra than to chip supply chains. The regime gate therefore permits only a half starter.

## Primary Evidence

- Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was $1.494 billion.
- Full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance is $6.8-$7.6 billion.
- Full-year free-cash-flow-before-growth guidance is $3.925-$4.725 billion.
- Vistra reported hedging of approximately 98% for 2026, 89% for 2027, and 65% for 2028.
- The May 1 SEC share count was 337.2 million; at $158.86, implied equity value is about
  $53.6 billion.
- March 31 long-term debt including current maturities was $19.163 billion, while preferred
  stock was $2.476 billion. Including cash, this implies an adjusted enterprise value near
  $74.4 billion and about 10.3x midpoint 2026 adjusted EBITDA.
- Midpoint FCFbG yield is about 8.1% before preferred dividends and approximately 7.7% after
  them. FCFbG is a company-defined non-GAAP measure that excludes growth spending.
- Management repurchased common shares at an average of $159.12 through May 1. That supports
  the entry area, but does not remove leverage or acquisition risk.
- Long-duration nuclear PPAs with AWS and Meta support a structural data-center demand thesis.

## Variant View

The market recognizes AI power scarcity, but still prices Vistra close to a conventional power
producer. The variant is that contracted, dispatchable generation deserves a higher and more
durable cash-flow multiple as data centers compete for reliable capacity.

## Risk And Sizing

The anti-thesis is not commodity noise alone. Debt, preferred claims, integration, regulation,
nuclear reliability, and PPA execution can destroy the expected rerating. Use a 4% policy cap,
enter no more than half now, and reserve the balance for $145-$150 or a clean August 7 review.
