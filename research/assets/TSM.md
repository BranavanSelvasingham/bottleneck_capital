## 3. Market-Implied View vs Variant View

What the market seems to believe:
- AI infrastructure scarcity matters, but many downstream/upstream beneficiaries now require 50x-plus earnings/guidance assumptions.
- TSMC deserves a premium because it owns the leading-edge manufacturing and advanced-packaging bottleneck, but geopolitics and customer concentration keep the position size constrained.

Our variant view:
- TSM is the cleaner, more durable long-term holding, while MU offers greater upside per dollar if memory scarcity persists; that distinction argues for holding TSM and buying MU only in its approved dip zone.
- The SA signal is useful for prioritization, not a trade instruction; the reported put exposure is a risk signal only.

Why we may be wrong:
- Geopolitical risk could overwhelm valuation and earnings quality.
- Customer concentration or AI accelerator cyclicality could make current growth less durable than it looks.

Is the variant view big enough to matter?
YES. The variant view is not that TSMC is unknown; it is that TSMC is the highest-quality upstream expression whose valuation is still acceptable relative to more stretched adjacent proxies.
