# Agentic Commerce

Sleeve: `agentic_commerce`

## Thesis

Widely adopted AI assistants and devices may increase commercial activity and demand for
payments, billing, identity, fraud management, settlement, and model-routing services.
The investment question is who retains incremental revenue and cash flow, not simply who
participates in an open protocol or announces an AI partnership.

## Consideration Candidates

- `STRIPE`: private-company research ID, not a confirmed public ticker. Decision is
  `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`; no investment or allocation is approved.

## Failure Test and Sizing

Open standards, merchant choice, competition, regulation, fee compression, acquisition costs,
and dilution may prevent value capture. Successful AI adoption is insufficient if transactions
only replace existing purchases or if the entry price already discounts the opportunity.

The sleeve has zero approved capital until instrument, financial, valuation, and capacity
reviews are complete. Factor and regime coefficients are provisional scenario assumptions,
not measured sensitivities; re-underwrite them before activating any allocation. This is
commerce and monetization exposure, not a direct hardware-supplier or frontier-model bet.
