# Frontier AI Platform

Sleeve: `frontier_ai_platform`

## Baseline Thesis

Frontier model developers can become scarce application and infrastructure platforms if model
quality, developer distribution, enterprise trust, and inference economics reinforce each other.
The upside is unusually large, but so are compute intensity, substitution, regulation, customer
concentration, governance, and valuation risk.

## Current Issuers

- `ANTHROPIC` - pre-IPO research issuer; not yet a tradable ticker.

## Decision Discipline

- Pre-IPO status is always `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`; no proxy ticker and no order.
- A public prospectus must be underwritten from audited figures, not revenue run-rate headlines.
- Fully diluted valuation, primary and secondary supply, lockups, governance, compute commitments,
  gross margin after compute, and free-cash-flow duration must be modeled before a buy decision.
- First-day scarcity is not intrinsic value. Use staged entry only after price discovery and the
  public-market decision is explicitly armed.
